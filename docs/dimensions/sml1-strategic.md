# SML 1 Strategic Sovereignty

*Strategic sovereignty evaluates the corporate governance architecture, ownership dependencies, and long-term continuity planning of the service provider.*

---

## Control Objectives & Controls

### SML 1.CO 1: Governance Authority
**Target State:** To ensure that individuals and entities with significant governance and decision-making authority are clearly defined and formally designated in order to fulfill their responsibilities.

> **Sovereignty Contribution:** Establishes strategic sovereignty by ensuring that control over service-related decisions is transparent, formally assigned, and free from undisclosed influence, enabling customers to understand who ultimately governs the service.

* **SML 1.CO 1.C 1** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  The entity exercising decisive control (and decision-making authority) over the service is clearly determined in terms of roles, bodies, and legal entities.  
  * **Audit Question:** Is the entity exercising decisive control and decision-making authority over the service clearly determined?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Initial` `Managed` `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

* **SML 1.CO 1.C 2** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  The entity exercising decisive control (and decision-making authority) over the service has assigned all responsible entities and individuals to their services and tasks, and has made this information accessible to stakeholders.  
  * **Audit Question:** Has the entity exercising decisive control and decision-making authority over the service formally assigned those responsible and informed affected entities, individuals and stakeholders about responsibilities for the services?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Managed` `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

* **SML 1.CO 1.C 3** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  Details of the nomination, assignment, services, and decision-making competencies regarding the related services are formally documented and accessible to all affected individuals and stakeholders.  
  * **Audit Question:** Are the nomination, assignment, services and decision-making competencies regarding the related services formally documented and accessible to all affected individuals and stakeholders?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

### SML 1.CO 2: Ownership and Control Structure
**Target State:** To ensure that ownership structures and controlling interests related to the service provider are transparent, identifiable, and do not introduce undisclosed external influence.

> **Sovereignty Contribution:** Strengthens strategic sovereignty by ensuring that ownership and control structures are transparent and that controlling interests can be clearly attributed, reducing the risk of hidden or external influence over the service.

* **SML 1.CO 2.C 1** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  The ownership structure of the service provider is documented and transparent.  
  * **Audit Question:** Is the ownership structure of the service provider documented and transparent?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Initial` `Managed` `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

* **SML 1.CO 2.C 2** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  Ultimate beneficial ownership (UBO) of the service provider is identified and documented.  
  * **Audit Question:** Is the ultimate beneficial ownership (UBO) of the service provider identified, documented, and within a jurisdiction listed in the AJL?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Managed` `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

---

### SML 1.CO 3: Strategic Alignment with Digital Sovereignty Objectives
**Target State:** To ensure that digital sovereignty objectives are explicitly defined and embedded in the strategy and decision-making processes related to the service.

> **Sovereignty Contribution:** Strengthens strategic sovereignty by ensuring that digital sovereignty is an intentional and managed objective, guiding decisions on architecture, providers, and dependencies rather than being incidental.

* **SML 1.CO 3.C 1** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  Digital sovereignty objectives are defined, integrated, and updated in the entity’s management policies to ensure digital sovereignty is considered in the implementation of the service or provider strategy at all levels.  
  * **Audit Question:** How is it ensured that digital sovereignty objectives are defined, integrated and updated in the entity’s management policies and considered in the implementation of the service or provider strategy?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Managed` `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

* **SML 1.CO 3.C 2** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  Strategic decisions related to the service consider digital sovereignty objectives.  
  * **Audit Question:** Are digital sovereignty objectives considered in strategic decisions related to the service?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

### SML 1.CO 4: Strategic Independence and Exit Capability
**Target State:** To ensure that dependencies on the services are considered at the entity’s strategic level and measures are in place to avoid disruptions in case of exits or substitution of the service.

> **Sovereignty Contribution:** Strengthens digital sovereignty by ensuring that dependencies are considered and that implications are monitored and actively managed to maintain the service quality.

* **SML 1.CO 4.C 1** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  The level of dependency on external providers relevant to the offered services is regularly assessed and updated with regard to its impact on independence and exit capability.  
  * **Audit Question:** How is the process designed to regularly define criteria and assess the level of dependency, while also considering the exit of contracted service providers where this affects the entity's own services?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Initial` `Managed` `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

* **SML 1.CO 4.C 2** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  Measures are in place to continuously detect and monitor how the external service providers' relevant activities impact or disrupt the services offered by the entity.  
  * **Audit Question:** Are measures in place to continuously detect and monitor the impact of external service providers?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Managed` `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

* **SML 1.CO 4.C 3** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  Measures for the substitution or exit of contracted external service providers are integrated into the decision-making process and design of processes that impact the provided services.  
  * **Audit Question:** How is it ensured that the substitution or exit of contracted external service providers is considered in decision-making and the design of processes that impact the services provided by the entity?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

---

### SML 1.CO 5: Accountability and Strategic Oversight
**Target State:** To ensure that accountability for digital sovereignty is clearly assigned and that oversight mechanisms are in place to monitor and enforce related objectives.

> **Sovereignty Contribution:** Strengthens strategic sovereignty by ensuring that digital sovereignty is actively managed, with clear accountability and oversight, rather than remaining an abstract or unowned objective.

* **SML 1.CO 5.C 1** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  Accountability for digital sovereignty is explicitly assigned to defined roles or management functions.  
  * **Audit Question:** Is accountability for digital sovereignty explicitly assigned to defined roles or management functions?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Managed` `Advanced` `Future-Proof`

* **SML 1.CO 5.C 2** | Level: *Governance & Operations* | Scope: *Service Provider* | Service Type: *All*  
  Mechanisms are in place to monitor and oversee digital sovereignty objectives at a strategic level.  
  * **Audit Question:** Are mechanisms in place to monitor and oversee digital sovereignty objectives at a strategic level?
  * **Maturity Levels:** `Future-Proof`
